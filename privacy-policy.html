1️⃣ Information We Collect

🔒 Important: All tools on AgentB24 run entirely in your browser. Text, images, passwords, and files you use in our tools are never sent to our servers and are never stored or logged by us.

Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use any tool on AgentB24. All tools are accessible without registration.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is automatically collected by third-party services we use (detailed below):

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and time/date of your visit.
  • Device Information: Device type, screen resolution, and browser settings.
  • Usage Data: Pages you view, time spent on pages, links you click, and other actions on the Site.
  • Cookie Data: Cookies and similar tracking technologies used by Adsterra and Google Analytics.

Information You Do NOT Need to Provide

You never need to provide personal information to use any of our tools. All tool processing (word counting, password generation, JSON formatting, image conversion, etc.) happens locally in your browser using JavaScript.

2️⃣ How We Use Your Information

The information collected automatically is used for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To understand how visitors use the Site (via Google Analytics)
  • To display relevant advertisements (via Adsterra)
  • To monitor and analyze usage trends and patterns
  • To detect and prevent technical issues and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for direct marketing without your explicit consent.

3️⃣ Adsterra & Advertising

AgentB24 uses Adsterra to display advertisements on the Site. Adsterra and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and improve ads shown on this website.

How Adsterra Works

  • Adsterra may load advertising scripts, banners, and native ad units.
  • Third-party advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure impressions, clicks, and ad performance.
  • Ad personalization and availability may vary based on your device, browser settings, region, and advertising partner policies.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of some third-party advertising cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page .

For more information on how Adsterra handles data, please visit: Adsterra Privacy Policy .

4️⃣ Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the Site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to our Site.

Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit the Site, rather than your name or other identifying information. We do not combine the information collected through Google Analytics with personally identifiable information.

Opting Out of Google Analytics

You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on .

5️⃣ Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. We use the following types of cookies:

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually only set in response to your actions such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms.
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): These allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site.
  • Advertising Cookies (Adsterra): These may be set by Adsterra or its advertising partners to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and reduce repeated or irrelevant ads.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, if you disable cookies, some features of the Site may not function properly.

For more information about managing cookies, visit AllAboutCookies.org .

6️⃣ Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect it. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

🛡️ All tool processing happens locally in your browser. Text you type, images you upload, passwords you generate, and any other data you input into our tools is processed entirely on your device using JavaScript. This data is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites. These links are provided for your convenience only. We have no control over the content of those sites and accept no responsibility for them or for any loss or damage that may arise from your use of them.

When you follow a link to an external website, please read the privacy policy of that website. This Privacy Policy only applies to our Site.

8️⃣ Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete that information.

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our records.

9️⃣ Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: The right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: The right to request that we delete your personal information ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Object: The right to object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell your personal information. However, if you are a California resident, you have the right to request this under the CCPA.
  • GDPR Rights (EU/EEA residents): You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.
