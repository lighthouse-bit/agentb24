📖 How to Add Chapters to a YouTube Video

1

Create Your Chapters

Add chapters using the tool above. The first chapter must start at 0:00. You need at least 3 chapters. Enter the timestamp and a short descriptive title for each section.

2

Copy the Chapter List

Click Copy All to copy the formatted chapter list. The format is automatically correct for YouTube.

3

Paste into YouTube Description

Go to your YouTube video → Edit → Description. Paste the chapter list anywhere in the description. Click Save and YouTube will automatically detect and display the chapters.

4

Wait for Processing

YouTube may take a few minutes to process the chapters after saving. Refresh the video page and the chapter markers should appear on the progress bar.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add chapters to a YouTube video?+
Add timestamps to your video description in the format 0:00 Chapter Title. The first timestamp must be 0:00 and you need at least 3 chapters. YouTube automatically detects them and adds chapter markers to the progress bar within a few minutes of saving.
What format does YouTube use for timestamps?+
Use M:SS or MM:SS for videos under an hour (e.g. 0:00, 1:30, 12:45) and H:MM:SS for longer videos (e.g. 1:05:30). The first timestamp must always start at 0:00.
How many chapters can a YouTube video have?+
YouTube supports up to 100 chapters per video. A minimum of 3 chapters is required for YouTube to recognize them. Each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long.
Do YouTube chapters help with SEO?+
Yes. Chapters appear as Key Moments in Google Search results, giving your video more visible links in search results. This can significantly increase click-through rates and discoverability. They also improve viewer experience and retention.
Why aren't my chapters showing up?+
The most common reasons chapters don't appear are: the first timestamp is not exactly 0:00, you have fewer than 3 chapters, a chapter is shorter than 10 seconds, or the timestamps are not in the correct format. Use this tool to generate a correctly formatted chapter list to avoid these issues.
