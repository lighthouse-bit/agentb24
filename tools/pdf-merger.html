Questions, answered

PDF merger FAQ

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?＋

No. Reading, arranging and generating the merged PDF happen locally inside your browser.

How do I merge only certain pages?＋

Use the page field on a PDF card. Enter all, a single page like 4, or ranges such as 1-3, 6, 9-12.

Can I rearrange my documents?＋

Yes. Drag each file card by its handle or use its up and down arrow buttons. The top-to-bottom order becomes the merged PDF order.

Why can’t an encrypted PDF be merged?＋

Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first. Use the PDF Protector & Unlocker, then return here with the unlocked copy.
