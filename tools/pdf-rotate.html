❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate pages in a PDF online? +
Upload your PDF, choose your rotation angle (90°, 180° or 270°), then click Rotate & Download PDF. The rotated file is generated and downloaded immediately — the whole process takes just seconds.
Is my PDF uploaded to a server? +
No — your PDF never leaves your device. All rotation processing happens locally in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Nothing is uploaded to any server at any point. Your documents are completely private.
Does rotating a PDF reduce the quality? +
Not at all. PDF rotation is a lossless operation — it only changes the page orientation metadata. All text, images, fonts and other content remain completely intact and at full quality.
Can I rotate only specific pages? +
This tool rotates all pages at once. To rotate specific pages, use the PDF Splitter to extract those pages, rotate them, then use the PDF Merger to combine everything back together.
What is the difference between 90° and 270° rotation? +
90° Clockwise turns the page to the right — portrait becomes landscape with the top going to the right.

270° Counter-clockwise turns the page to the left — portrait becomes landscape with the top going to the left. It is equivalent to 90° in the opposite direction.
Why is my PDF displaying sideways or upside down? +
PDFs created from scanned documents often have incorrect orientation metadata depending on how the paper was fed into the scanner. PDFs exported from certain applications may also have unexpected rotation. This tool fixes the orientation with a single click.
