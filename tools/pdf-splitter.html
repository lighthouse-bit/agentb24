Questions, answered

PDF splitter FAQ

Does AgentB24 upload my PDF?＋

No. Your browser reads and processes the document locally. This tool does not send it to AgentB24 or another processing server.

How do I select specific pages?＋

Click individual thumbnails, use All, None, Odd or Even, or type a range such as 1-3, 5, 8-10 and click Apply.

Why are separate pages delivered as a ZIP?＋

A ZIP keeps every generated PDF together and prevents your browser from blocking a long series of automatic downloads.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?＋

Protected documents must be unlocked first. Use the PDF Protector & Unlocker, then return with the unlocked copy.
