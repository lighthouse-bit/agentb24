Questions, answered

Video compressor FAQ

Why does compression take the full video duration?＋

The browser plays, resizes and records the video frame by frame. This keeps processing local but makes it approximately real time.

Why does the output use WebM?＋

WebM can be encoded natively through MediaRecorder. Reliable local MP4 creation would require a much larger transcoding engine.

Will the compressed file always be smaller?＋

No. Lower settings usually reduce size, but a short or already optimized source may produce an equal or larger WebM file. The result panel shows the actual comparison.

What settings should I choose?＋

Start with Balanced. Choose Small file for messaging or slow connections, and High quality when preserving detail matters more than size.
