📖 How to Generate a Professional Email

1

Choose a Category & Template

Select a category tab (Work & HR, Job & Career, Business, or Customer), then click the email template that matches your situation.

2

Fill In Your Details

Enter your name, the recipient's name, and any other specific details the template requires. All fields are optional — leave any blank and a placeholder will appear.

3

Choose a Tone

Select your preferred email tone — Formal, Polite, Assertive, or Friendly. The tone adjusts the opening, closing, and phrasing of the email.

4

Generate, Edit & Copy

Click Generate Email. Edit the result directly in the text box if needed, then copy the email body or subject line to paste into your email client.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are these email templates free?+
Yes — completely free. No sign-up, no account, no limits. Generate as many emails as you need across all templates.
Can I edit the generated email?+
Yes. The output appears in an editable text area. Make any changes you need before copying — add details, adjust wording, or personalize further.
Is my information stored?+
No. Everything runs entirely in your browser. The names and details you type are never sent to any server. Your information is completely private.
What email templates are available?+
Over 15 templates across 4 categories: Work & HR (sick leave, resignation, WFH request, apology), Job & Career (interview follow-up, salary negotiation, offer acceptance/decline, networking), Business (meeting request, project proposal, price increase, payment reminder), Customer (refund request, formal complaint, subscription cancellation).
What does the tone option change?+
The tone adjusts the greeting, phrasing, and sign-off of the email. Formal uses strict professional language. Polite is warm but professional. Assertive is direct and confident. Friendly is casual and approachable.
