📖 What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is the standard placeholder text used by designers, developers, and publishers worldwide. It allows creators to focus on layout, typography, and visual design without the distraction of meaningful text.

The text originates from "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" by the Roman philosopher Cicero, written in 45 BC. It has been used as standard filler text since the 1500s, and became widely popular in the desktop publishing era.

The standard opening passage — "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit..." — is recognized globally and signals to viewers that the content is a placeholder.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum? +
Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text widely used in graphic design, publishing, and web development. It fills space in layouts so designers can visualize a finished product before real content is written.
Where does Lorem Ipsum come from? +
Lorem Ipsum is derived from "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" by the ancient Roman philosopher Cicero, written in 45 BC. The text has been used as standard placeholder text since the 1500s when an unknown printer scrambled it to create a type specimen book.
Can I generate Lorem Ipsum as HTML? +
Yes! Select HTML Paragraphs in the Format dropdown. The output will be wrapped in <p> tags, ready to paste directly into your HTML file or CMS.
How many paragraphs can I generate? +
You can generate up to 100 paragraphs, 500 sentences, or 1000 words in one click. All generation happens instantly in your browser with no limits on how many times you can generate.
Is Lorem Ipsum real Latin? +
Lorem Ipsum is derived from Latin but has been deliberately scrambled and altered to be non-meaningful. It is not intended to be read as real text — it is designed to look like natural language while being intentionally unreadable.
Why do designers use Lorem Ipsum? +
Designers use Lorem Ipsum because readable content distracts reviewers from evaluating the visual design. Placeholder text lets stakeholders focus on typography, spacing, and layout rather than reading and editing the content.
