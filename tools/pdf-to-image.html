Questions, answered

PDF to image FAQ

Should I use PNG or JPG?＋

PNG preserves sharp text and graphics without compression artifacts. JPG is usually smaller and works well for photographs and scanned pages.

Can I convert selected pages only?＋

Yes. Enter all, a page like 4, or ranges such as 1-3, 6, 9-12.

Are protected PDFs supported?＋

Yes, when you enter the correct password. The password is used locally and is never transmitted.
