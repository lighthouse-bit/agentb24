Questions, answered

Audio trimmer FAQ

Which audio files can I trim?＋

Any format decoded by your browser can work. Common formats include MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG and WebM audio.

Why is the output WAV?＋

PCM WAV can be generated directly and reliably in the browser. MP3 or AAC export requires a separate codec library or server-side conversion.

What does normalization do?＋

It measures the highest sample in your selection and adjusts gain so the peak reaches approximately −1 dB without intentional clipping.

Does trimming reduce quality?＋

The selected audio is decoded and written as uncompressed PCM. It avoids additional lossy compression, though it does not restore quality already lost in the source.
