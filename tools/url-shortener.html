LINKS, WITHOUT THE CLUTTER

How to shorten a URL

  1. Copy the full address of the page you want to share.
  2. Paste it above, then choose an ending and expiry if you want.
  3. Create your short link. Copy it or download its QR code.

For campaign tracking, add your parameters with the UTM Builder first. The shortener preserves the destination’s query parameters and fragment.

A few useful answers

Will my link work after I close this page?

Yes. Links are saved on our server. They work until their selected expiry, unless disabled or the service becomes unavailable. Keep your original URL as a backup.

Can I change a destination later?

Not currently. Create a new short link for a different destination. Custom endings are unique and cannot be reassigned, including after expiry.

Is there a limit?

To limit abuse, creation is capped at 10 links per minute and 100 per day per network address. Shared networks may reach the limit sooner.

Are shortened destinations checked for safety?

We validate URL format but do not certify destinations as safe. Only open links from people you trust. Don’t use this service for spam, phishing or harmful content.
