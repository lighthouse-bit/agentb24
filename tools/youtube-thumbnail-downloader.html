❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?+
Paste any YouTube video URL into the input field, click Get Thumbnails, then click the Download button under your preferred resolution. The thumbnail saves to your device immediately.
What thumbnail resolutions are available?+
YouTube generates thumbnails in four sizes: Maximum Resolution (1280×720 HD), High Quality (480×360), Medium Quality (320×180), and Standard Definition (120×90). Not every video has all four resolutions available.
Why is the HD thumbnail showing as unavailable?+
Not all YouTube videos have a maximum resolution thumbnail. Older videos or those uploaded in lower quality may only have standard definition thumbnails. Try downloading the highest quality that is available.
Does this work with YouTube Shorts?+
Yes. The tool works with YouTube Shorts, standard videos, and youtu.be short links. Simply paste any valid YouTube URL and the thumbnail will be extracted automatically.
Is it legal to download YouTube thumbnails?+
Thumbnails are publicly accessible images. Downloading them for personal reference, analysis, or inspiration is generally accepted. However, do not redistribute or use another creator's thumbnail commercially without their permission as it may infringe copyright.
