📖 How YouTube Ad Revenue Works

1

Advertisers Pay CPM

Advertisers bid on ad placements. CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the amount they pay per 1,000 ad impressions. This varies hugely by niche — finance pays $15–$30, gaming pays $1–$4.

2

YouTube Takes 45%

YouTube keeps 45% of all ad revenue. The creator receives 55%. This results in the RPM (Revenue Per Mille) — the actual amount a creator earns per 1,000 views.

3

Not Every View Has an Ad

Ad blockers, skipped ads, and short watch times mean not every view generates revenue. The CTR slider accounts for this — typically 40–60% of views generate ad income.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?+
YouTube pays creators through RPM (Revenue Per Mille). Typical RPM ranges from $1–$3 for entertainment/gaming channels up to $15–$29 for finance, insurance, and legal content. The average across all niches is approximately $3–$5 per 1,000 views.
What is the difference between CPM and RPM?+
CPM (Cost Per Mille) is what advertisers pay YouTube per 1,000 ad impressions.
RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is what the creator earns per 1,000 video views after YouTube's 45% cut. RPM is always lower than CPM since not every view gets an ad.
Which niche pays the most on YouTube?+
The highest-paying YouTube niches by CPM are: Finance & Investing ($15–$30), Insurance & Legal ($12–$25), Business & Entrepreneurship ($10–$20), Health & Medicine ($8–$18), Technology ($5–$12), and Education ($4–$10). Entertainment and Gaming typically pay $1–$4 CPM.
How do I increase my YouTube earnings?+
To increase earnings: target higher-CPM niches, attract audiences from high-paying countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia), make longer videos (more ad placements), enable all ad formats, and diversify with channel memberships, Super Thanks, merchandise, and sponsorships.
Are these earnings estimates accurate?+
These are estimates based on typical industry averages. Actual earnings vary significantly based on audience geography, ad engagement, seasonality, video length, and whether the channel is monetized. Use these figures as a rough guide, not guaranteed income.
