Questions, answered
Temporary email FAQ
How long does this inbox last?+
The inbox credentials remain available for the life of this browser tab, and the page restores them after a refresh. Mail.tm currently states that received messages are stored for seven days.
Can I send or reply to messages?+
No. This is a receive-only disposable inbox.
Why is a verification email missing?+
Allow a minute and refresh. Some services block known disposable domains, in which case a different address may help but is not guaranteed.
Is message HTML safe to open?+
HTML messages are isolated in a restricted frame. Scripts, forms and remote resources are blocked to reduce tracking and unsafe behavior.
