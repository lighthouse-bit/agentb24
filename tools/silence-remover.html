Questions, answered

Silence remover FAQ

How is silence detected?＋

The tool measures short audio blocks. Blocks below your dB threshold for at least the minimum duration are marked as silence.

Why keep padding?＋

Padding preserves a small amount of room tone around speech and helps avoid cutting the beginnings or endings of words.

Does this upload my recording?＋

No. Decoding, analysis, joining and WAV export all happen in your browser.
