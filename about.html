📖 Our Story

Why we built AgentB24 and what drives us every day.

AgentB24 was born out of a simple frustration: too many online tools are either behind paywalls, riddled with intrusive ads, require you to create an account, or upload your private files to someone else's server.

We believe the most useful tools — the ones people use every single day — should be free, fast, and private. That's why every tool on AgentB24 runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device. No accounts. No uploads. No hidden costs.

What started as a small collection of developer utilities has grown into a comprehensive toolkit used by thousands of people around the world — from students checking word counts to developers formatting JSON, from designers picking color palettes to business owners generating QR codes for their restaurants.

We're just getting started. New tools are added regularly, and every decision we make is guided by one question: "Does this genuinely help someone?"

✅ Why Choose AgentB24?

What makes us different from other online tool sites.

🔒

Your Data Never Leaves Your Browser

Unlike most online tools that process your data on their servers, every AgentB24 tool runs 100% in your browser. Your passwords, text, images, and files are never uploaded anywhere. This is especially critical for sensitive data like passwords and private documents.

⚡

Instant Results — No Loading Screens

Because all processing happens locally, there is no waiting for a server to respond. Results appear in milliseconds. Even on a slow internet connection, the tools perform at full speed.

💡 How AgentB24 Is Free

Transparency about how we sustain the site.

AgentB24 is completely free for users. We do not charge subscriptions, sell premium plans, or charge per use. We keep the site free through Adsterra — the same advertising platform used by millions of websites across the internet.

Ads are displayed in designated areas — top banners, in-content areas, and sidebars. We deliberately avoid pop-ups, interstitials, and auto-playing ads that disrupt your experience. The ads are relevant and clearly marked as advertisements.

We appreciate your understanding. Every visit and every click on a relevant ad helps us keep the site running and allows us to build more tools. If you find AgentB24 useful, the best way to support us is simply to keep using it and share it with others.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AgentB24.

Is AgentB24 really free forever? +
Yes. All tools on AgentB24 are free to use with no limitations. We sustain the site through Adsterra advertising. There are no premium plans, no paywalls, and no paid tiers. Every feature is available to every user at no cost.
Do you store any data I enter into the tools? +
No. All tool processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type, paste, or upload is ever sent to our servers. We have no ability to see what you enter into any tool. Your data is 100% private.
Can I suggest a new tool? +
Absolutely! We love suggestions. Email us at hello@AgentB24.com with your idea. We review every suggestion and prioritize the most-requested tools for future development.
Can I use AgentB24 offline? +
Most of our tools work offline after the page has loaded, since all processing is done in JavaScript with no server calls required. You need an internet connection for the initial page load and for tools that use third-party libraries (like the QR Code Generator).
How do you make money? +
We display non-intrusive ads through Adsterra. Ad revenue covers our hosting costs and funds continued development of new tools. We do not sell user data, run affiliate schemes, or offer paid plans. Ads are our only source of revenue.
How often do you add new tools? +
We aim to release new tools regularly. Our roadmap includes tools for text manipulation, developer utilities, SEO analysis, image processing, math calculators, and much more. Follow us or bookmark the homepage to see new additions as they launch.
