ℹ️ About This Dashboard

Records are stored on your hosting server. Browser storage identifies returning browsers; clearing storage or using another device creates a different visitor ID.

Dates use this browser's timezone. Sessions group page views by browser activity; time on page measures visible-tab time, sampled every 30 seconds. Geography is an approximate IP lookup from ipapi.co; blocked or unavailable lookups remain Unknown.

Historical records from the previous tracker may contain duplicates, shared IP identities, or missing time measurements. Earlier records deleted by its 5,000-record limit cannot be recovered here.
